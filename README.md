# Research: Brandie Janow

A public repository for selected independent research, datasets, methods, signals, and research outputs by Brandie Janow.

Private source material, unpublished research, confidential records, and restricted participant material are not published here unless explicitly approved for public release.

Website: https://www.brandiejanow.com/

## Editorial writing style

Do not use em dashes in website copy, research writing, public data labels, metadata, or documentation. Use commas, colons, parentheses, or full stops as appropriate. Preserve the intended meaning and source attribution when revising historical text. Treat this as the standard for future updates.

## Publication review standard

Public copy describes research, methods, participants, evidence, dates and findings in clear, direct sentences. Use American English, affirmative wording and varied sentence structures. Keep the focus on information that readers can verify or investigate. Original quotations, formal institution names, historical event titles, citations and URLs retain their source wording. Review public pages, search descriptions, data labels and publication summaries before each release.


## Reference for the research website's presentation

The academic website of [Mohammed Mashary Alnaim](https://mohaalnaim.lovable.app/) provides a useful example of organizing an individual research catalogue. Its navigation separates research areas, a complete bibliography, biography and consultancy. Relevant papers are displayed beside the subjects they address, allowing readers to move from an inquiry to its published evidence.

When developing this research website, use the following criteria: research programmes clearly separated from individual studies; accurate publication dates and authors; direct journal or DOI references; source and method information; readable typography; accessible page titles; and consistent navigation. The original research and editorial language remain Brandie Janow's. This is a structural reference for review, with independent academic verification for cited papers.
